Who We Are
A 3 acre Nature Event Venue

Arekas Arena is a 3 acre nature event venue operated by KR ENDEAVOURS in Rajanukunte, North Bangalore. The venue was created for weddings, engagements, birthdays, corporate events, private gatherings and family celebrations.

Set amidst sprawling arecanut palms, bamboo groves, and a serene koi pond, Arekas Arena offers an escape from the urban rush — open lawns, a bamboo dining pavilion and a stage built for celebrations that use nature as the backdrop, not a banquet hall.
