Cancellation & Refund Policy

At Arekas, we understand that plans can change. Our policy is designed to be fair to both our guests and our team.

Home › Cancellation & Refund Policy
Arekas Arena
1
Arekas Arena (Event Venue)

Due to the high demand for specific dates and the extensive preparation required for our bamboo stage and koi pond area, the following applies:

Cancellation Window Refund Status
Booking Deposit Non-Refundable (50% advance)
30+ Days Before Event Deposit non-refundable; catering/decor extras may be refunded
Within 30 Days of Event 100% Non-Refundable
Rescheduling: Requests to reschedule an event must be made at least 45 days in advance and are subject to date availability.
Bamboo Arcadia
2
Arekas Bamboo Arcadia (Cottages)

For individual room or cottage bookings, our policy is as follows:

Cancellation Window Refund
7 or More Days Before Check-in 100% Refund (minus processing fee)
3 to 7 Days Before Check-in 50% Refund
Within 72 Hours / No Show No Refund
Early Departure No Refund
3
Force Majeure (Act of God)

In the event of extreme weather, natural disasters, or government-mandated lockdowns that make the farm inaccessible or unsafe, Arekas reserves the right to cancel bookings. In such cases:

Guests will be offered a full credit note valid for 12 months or a full refund, at the management's discretion.
4
Refund Processing
  • All eligible refunds will be processed within 7–10 business days of the cancellation approval.
  • Refunds will be credited back to the original payment method — Bank Transfer, UPI, or Credit/Debit Card.
5
Damage & Final Payments
  • Arekas Arena: You may be required to pay 100% of the amount before the start of the event, provided no damage occurred to the bamboo structures, koi pond, or landscape.
  • Arekas Bamboo Arcadia: Any significant damage to cottage interiors or furniture will be charged to the guest's card on file or deducted from their deposit.
