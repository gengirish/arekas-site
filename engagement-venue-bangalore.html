Engagements
A Nature Backdrop for Your Ring Ceremony

An engagement is often smaller and more intimate than a wedding, and Arekas Arena's open lawn and bamboo stage work equally well at that scale — a garden-style ring ceremony for 50–150 guests, or a larger engagement party that uses the full 3 acre property. The same infrastructure as our wedding venue is available, sized to your guest count.
