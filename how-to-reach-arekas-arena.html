Driving Directions
Getting Here by Road

Arekas Arena is in Arekere Village, Rajanukunte, Yelahanka Taluk, Bengaluru 562163 — north of Yelahanka, in North Bangalore. Most guests approach via Bellary Road / NH44 through Yelahanka and continue towards Rajanukunte. Since exact turns depend on where you're starting from, we recommend using the Google Maps link above for live, turn-by-turn directions and an accurate travel-time estimate from your location — we'd rather point you to a live map than guess at drive times that vary with traffic.
