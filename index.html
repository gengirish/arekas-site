Frequently Asked

Questions About Arekas Arena

Is Arekas Arena a wedding venue in Bangalore?
Yes. Arekas Arena is a nature-focused outdoor wedding venue in Rajanukunte, North Bangalore. The 3 acre property includes an open lawn, bamboo dining pavilion, stage, cottages, swimming pool and other outdoor spaces suitable for weddings and celebrations.
How many guests can Arekas Arena accommodate?
Arekas Arena can accommodate events of up to 500 guests. The dining pavilion can seat more than 100 guests and accommodate approximately 250–300 guests in buffet or cocktail-style arrangements.
How much does Arekas Arena cost?
Venue packages start at ₹1,80,000 for 12 hours for up to 500 guests, and ₹3,50,000 for 24 hours for up to 500 guests. See our full pricing page for what's included and additional charges.
Where is Arekas Arena located?
Arekas Arena is located in Arekere Village, Rajanukunte, Yelahanka Taluk, Bengaluru, Karnataka 562163 — close to Yelahanka and easily reachable from Doddaballapur, Jakkur, Hebbal, Devanahalli, Thanisandra and Vidyaranyapura. See how to reach us.
Does Arekas Arena have accommodation?
Yes. The property has four couple cottages and two dormitory cottages, with accommodation for approximately 30 guests.
Does Arekas Arena have a swimming pool?
Yes. The property has a swimming pool available to guests.
Is Arekas Arena suitable for corporate events?
Yes. The property is suitable for corporate events, team-building activities, workshops, conferences, product launches and corporate outings.
Can Arekas Arena host a 300-person outdoor wedding?
Yes. With capacity for up to 500 guests across a 3 acre outdoor property, a 300-person wedding fits comfortably at Arekas Arena.
Is Arekas Arena a good birthday party venue in North Bangalore?
Yes. Arekas Arena hosts both kids' and adult birthday celebrations, with a swimming pool, farm activities, open lawns and a dining pavilion — see our birthday venue page for details.
Can I host a corporate team outing at Arekas Arena?
Yes. Arekas Arena is used for team outings, offsites, workshops and conferences, with open-air breakout spaces and on-site accommodation for overnight offsites.
Can wedding guests stay overnight at Arekas Arena?
Yes. The 24-hour venue package includes complimentary accommodation, and the property's four couple cottages and two dormitories can host wedding guests overnight.
Can I host a private party in a farm setting at Arekas Arena?
Yes. Arekas Arena's open lawns, bamboo groves and dining pavilion suit private parties and get-togethers of varying sizes in a genuine farm setting.
What is included in Arekas Arena's event packages?
Every package includes a Nadasaram team, Dhol team, event planners and an event host, with 50% advance booking. See the pricing page for full details.
Does Arekas Arena provide catering, decoration and photography?
Yes, these can be arranged as add-ons. Indicative charges: buffet catering around ₹490 per plate, basic decoration from ₹42,000, and photography from ₹25,000 — final pricing depends on your package and requirements.
Does Arekas Arena have parking?
Yes. Arekas Arena provides on-site parking for guest vehicles as part of the 3 acre property.
Is Arekas Arena a good farm wedding venue near Bangalore?
Yes. Arekas Arena is one of the few venues near Bangalore that combines a genuine working farm setting — arecanut palms, bamboo groves and open lawns — with full wedding infrastructure and on-site accommodation.
Can I combine an event with an overnight cottage stay at Arekas Arena?
Yes. Guests can combine an event at Arekas Arena with a stay at Arekas Bamboo Arcadia, our adjoining farm-stay cottages, for multi-day celebrations.
What is Arekas Bamboo Arcadia?
Arekas Bamboo Arcadia is our farm-stay brand — handcrafted bamboo cottages on the same property, offering a nature retreat separate from event hosting. Learn more.
How do I book Arekas Arena?
Message us on WhatsApp at +91 89511 26633 or use the enquiry form on this page with your event date, guest count and requirements, and our team will get back to you with availability and a package recommendation.
