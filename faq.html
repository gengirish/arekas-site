General
General Questions
Is Arekas Arena a wedding venue in Bangalore?
Yes. Arekas Arena is a nature-focused outdoor wedding venue in Rajanukunte, North Bangalore. The 3 acre property includes an open lawn, bamboo dining pavilion, stage, cottages, swimming pool and other outdoor spaces suitable for weddings and celebrations.
How many guests can Arekas Arena accommodate?
Arekas Arena can accommodate events of up to 500 guests. The dining pavilion can seat more than 100 guests and accommodate approximately 250–300 guests in buffet or cocktail-style arrangements.
Where is Arekas Arena located?
Arekas Arena is located in Arekere Village, Rajanukunte, Yelahanka Taluk, Bengaluru, Karnataka 562163 — close to Yelahanka and reachable from Doddaballapur, Jakkur, Hebbal, Devanahalli, Thanisandra and Vidyaranyapura.
Does Arekas Arena have accommodation?
Yes. The property has four couple cottages and two dormitory cottages, with accommodation for approximately 30 guests.
Does Arekas Arena have a swimming pool?
Yes. The property has a swimming pool available to guests.
Is Arekas Arena suitable for corporate events?
Yes. The property is suitable for corporate events, team-building activities, workshops, conferences, product launches and corporate outings.
Does Arekas Arena have parking?
Yes. Arekas Arena provides on-site parking for guest vehicles as part of the 3 acre property.
What facilities does Arekas Arena have?
The property has a swimming pool, on-site temple, bamboo groves, koi pond, bonfire area, open lawn, stage, pergola and a bamboo dining pavilion.

Pricing
Pricing Questions
How much does Arekas Arena cost?
Venue packages start at ₹1,80,000 for 12 hours for up to 500 guests, and ₹3,50,000 for 24 hours for up to 500 guests.
What is included in Arekas Arena's event packages?
Every package includes full venue access, a Nadasaram team, Dhol team, event planners and an event host, with 50% advance booking.
Does the price include catering and decoration?
No — catering, decoration and photography are add-ons. Buffet catering runs from about ₹490 per plate, decoration from ₹42,000, and photography from ₹25,000.
Is accommodation included in the price?
Accommodation is complimentary with the 24-hour package. With the 12-hour package, it is charged separately.
What is the cost of a 500-person outdoor event venue in Bangalore?
At Arekas Arena, hosting up to 500 guests is included in both the 12-hour (₹1,80,000) and 24-hour (₹3,50,000) packages, with no extra per-guest charge up to that capacity.
