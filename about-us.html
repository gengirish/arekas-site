Who We Are
A Nature Destination Built for Memorable Events

Arekas Arena is a beautiful, farm-centric, nature-crafted event destination nestled in the lush outskirts of Bangalore. Set amidst sprawling arecanut palms, bamboo groves, and a serene koi pond, Arekas offers an escape from the urban rush — a place where nature becomes the venue.

Whether you are hosting an intimate birthday celebration, a grand wedding reception, a corporate team outing, or a weekend retreat among bamboo cottages, Arekas brings together the beauty of the outdoors and the warmth of thoughtful hospitality.
