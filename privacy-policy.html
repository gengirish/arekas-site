Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information across Arekas Arena and Arekas Bamboo Arcadia.

Home › Privacy Policy
Ⓘ
Welcome

Welcome to Arekas.in. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information across our two primary business divisions: Arekas Arena (Event Hosting) and Arekas Bamboo Arcadia (Bamboo Arcadia).

1
Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to all our guests and clients. This includes:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, and postal address.
  • Booking Details: Dates of stay at Bamboo Arcadia, event dates for Arekas Arena, number of guests, and specific event requirements.
  • Identification: Government-issued ID (required for check-ins at Bamboo Arcadia and certain event contracts).
  • Payment Data: Transaction details, billing address, and payment confirmation. We do not store full credit card numbers; these are handled by our payment processors.
2
How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: Processing your cottage bookings, managing event venue schedules, and coordinating catering or decor for Arekas Arena.
  • Communication: Sending booking confirmations, updates regarding your event, or responding to inquiries.
  • Safety & Security: Maintaining guest registers as required by local regulations and ensuring the security of our farm-stay and venue premises.
  • Marketing: With your consent, we may send updates about seasonal offers at Bamboo Arcadia or new facilities at Arekas Arena.
3
Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We only share information when:

  • Service Providers: We work with third-party vendors (e.g., payment gateways, booking engines, or event coordinators) who assist in our operations.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by Indian law or government authorities (e.g., local police guest registration forms).
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger or sale of the business, your data may be transferred to the new owner.
4
Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5
Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request the correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request the deletion of your data (subject to legal or contractual retention requirements).
  • Opt-out of marketing communications at any time.
6
Cookies and Tracking

Our website, arekas.in, may use cookies to enhance your browsing experience, analyze site traffic, and remember your preferences for future visits. You can manage cookie settings through your browser.
